WHAT DO I NEED TO COMPLETE YOUR COMMISSION

I need at least one of the following:

  • A reference sheet containing visual representation of your character for at minimum as much of it as will be included in the image i will be drawing for you (e.g. for a fullbody I need a full body reference)
  • A detailed description of your character so I know what I'm supposed to draw (any important features, colours, just anything I wouldn't automatically know I suppose). This should also include what you want me to draw your character doing, if you have a preference for that. I will send you a sketch of what I think your character looks like so you can correct me if I get something wrong before I do the full drawing anyway.

I would also appreciate if you could send me a rough sketch of what you want your character to do, if you have a particular idea. This would let me get straight to work and make your commission take less time! It can literally be stick figures and it would still help, promise!

IMPORTANT!!!!

Once you have sent me your reference image(s) and/or character description, I will draw a sketch of your commission and send it to you for confirmation that I have gotten it right. I will then repeat this process for any additional development stages (e.g. refined sketch, lineart, colour, shading). This way, I can fix any issues you have during the process instead of afterwards, to save time.
