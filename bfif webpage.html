What is BFIF?

BFIF is my object show!! I began work on it in late 2023, and it has continued to this day!

BFIF has sort of a strange structure. It began with a twitter post asking people if they wanted to battle for dream island, and sort of ballooned into the show it is now! It was a complete accident, which is a little scary! But anyway, that's not too important.

Essentially, it started with a group of 25 people competing in a challenge in a discord server (roleplaying), then I would write a script out of that challenge and animate it. Note that this is why it sometimes feels a little all over the place, lol. Anyway nowadays most of the contestants sort of silently left the show and I kind of had to adopt all their characters. I have like 10 adopted object children which I cannot do anything with for fear of that being against the wishes of their creators. uh. yeah

Nowadays the show is mainly just 1 evening of me roleplaying 20 characters while like 5 people who stayed roleplay theirs, then I write a script out of it, storyboard it, animate it, yknow. All that stuff. All on my own ! I do get help sometimes with writing and voice acting though. Look for more details on that in the Crew section!

As of right now, the most recent episode is BFIF 3, but I am hard at work on BFIF 4! It will be out as soon as I can finish it, I animate for several hours daily I am not joking it has been over a year of this

Powerbook Team

Powerbook

He/They

Powerbook is the team leader of Team Powerbook (obviously). He has the highest death count out of anyone so far, I think! I mean I dunno, I haven't checked... Anyway, he's a little loud and obnoxious at times, but his team still respects his leadership. Even if he doesn't actually do much.

Tophat

He/Him

Tophat is the only contestant with prior experience in a competition! He won an object camp in the past, and likes to brag about it. Other than that, though, he is a competent and creative competitor and does his best to organise the team during challenges.

Bindery

He/They

Bindery is fairly quiet, and doesn't like to make himself known much. But when he's brought into being a main competitor of a challenge, he is a voice of reason to counterbalance Tophat's more crazy ideas.

Eye Sticker

They/It

Eye Sticker is quite quiet and prefers to keep to themself mostly. However, when it does get involved in conversation, they are quite hyperactive and enjoy sharing information with others.

Stumpy

He/Him

Stumpy is also rather quiet, but he's not as uncaring or sophisticated as he first appears. He overthinks quite a bit about things at times, too.

Arsonists Team

Brock

He/Him

Brock has been the unofficial leader of Arsonists Team so far, in the sense that he is the main driver in completing the challenges, at the cost of being quite serious and unfun. Unfortunately, his team failed in BFIF 3, putting his position as team leader in jeopardy! Will he get out? Who knows...

Popsicle

He/Him

Popsicle quite likes the cold! He hasn't really done anything yet though. Sorry guys. He melted in BFIF 3 so I guess that was something?

Dreamcatcher

He/She/It

Dreamcatcher LOVES fire!! And other generally chaotic things. Basically she greatly enjoys chaos.

Lake/Kruz

L: He/Him K: They/Them

Lake and Kruz are the sole two alters in the system collectively known as 'Diamond'. No one really refers to them as a collective though, as they are both very distinct personalities. Lake is more reserved and skittish, he prefers to avoid conflict and to find a way to disengage from anything dangerous. Kruz, in comparison, is much more violent and defensive. They want to fight off any threats, and will fiercely defend the system whenever they can. However, that hotheadedness might alienate them from the other contestants (and Lake).

Fishbone

She/They

Fishbone is quite meek, and doesn't speak up much. Since she has less strong of a personality than the others, she doesn't have much opportunity to contribute to challenges, so is always looking for a way to butt in and make their presence known. However, when she gets that chance, she often messes up and stutters so people never really pay much attention to her. A shame, really.

Edibles Team

Red Lollipop

He/Him

Red Lollipop is the team leader of Edibles Team. He can be a little pushy, and takes charge of any situations he's in. Since most of his teammates don't want to push back, he tends to treat them as expendable on occasion.

Fizz

He/She

Fizz has been quite upset since Mug got out. She's resolved to do his best for the team, though, and is starting to gain confidence back.

Mic

She/They

Mic has quite a mysterious past. Who was she before the competition? She'd prefer to keep that hidden for now. In challenges though, she is mostly meek like the other contestants, but is vocal whenever something bad might happen to another teammate.

Tube Thing

She/It

Tube Thing hasn't really done much yet, but thats fairly fitting, as most of her team don't really know what to do with her. It's very off-putting, and tends to just sort of sit and let things happen to itself during most situations. Who knows what she could be thinking about?

Mug

He/She

Mug hasn't really shown up much since her elimination, so there's not much to say about him really. He's friends with Fizz and likes to draw !

Total Vibes Team

Pizza

He/Him

Pizza is the team leader of Total Vibes Team (despite Crisps' protests). He pushes the rest of his team to be chill and do essentially nothing all challenge. Unluckily for him, his only supporter has been eliminated, so he must compete with Crisps over who is directing the rest for the challenges. Despite his perpetuation of the value of being 'chill', he's been getting more and more expressive and emotional. I wonder where he'll end up?

Crisps

He/Him

Crisps started off fairly shy, being the last to be picked for the team, but over the past few challenges has grown to be strong and capable, to the point where he has been able to fight Pizza's leadership to make the team win a challenge! Additionally, he's found motivation in something (or someone) else...

JPad

He/Him

The youngest contestant by far, JPad only really is competing because he's the little brother of Headphony. He enjoys playing cube-based rhythm games and incremental games. His favourite snacks include gummy worms and chocolate milk. He brings to Total Vibes Team the ability to 'buy' items and have them spontaneously generate right in front of him. Unfortunately though, Pizza is against letting him use this power, as it would be against the spirit of the team, apparently.

Disqualified

It/Its

Disqualified prefers to keep to itself mostly. However, it has been starting to befriend JPad, which is nice. It also has a yet-to-be-seen violent streak, partially due to the very large spikes it has.

Snail Guide

She/He

Snail Guide was really the only one on Pizza's side, mainly because he liked her snail-creation powers. However, now she's eliminated, she's had more opportunity to reflect on Pizza, and might feel differently.
