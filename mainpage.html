Who am I?

My name is W! I am the host alter of a system, and am a fictive from the show Battle For Dream Island. The other alter I am aware of is Two (another fictive from the same show). We both go by they/them pronouns! This website is being made by me (W) though lol

I am interested in several popular medias, currently mainly object shows! (such as BFDI, II, TNM, ONE, EBS, etc). I am also a big fan of UT/DR, TADC, and Smiling Friends. Unfortunately like half of these are ending or already have so expect updates to this I suppose. I also on occasion enjoy tidbits of MLP or Steven Universe, in managed doses.

As for animation, although I have had no official training, I have made many animations! You can check my personal YouTube channel out here: W Variable

If you would like to see where I put most of my 'professional' work, check out my Object Show BFIF! It is a competition show set in the same (or a very similar) universe to Battle For Dream Island, with a focus on worldbuilding and filling in the gaps in things that BFDI does not acknowledge. Or, it will be, once I make it to episode 5!!! So, it basically takes forever to make episodes :( I'm working on it though!! But yes please check it out if you can, it would mean a lot to me.
